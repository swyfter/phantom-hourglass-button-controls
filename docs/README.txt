The Legend of Zelda: Phantom Hourglass - Button Controls Patch v2.1.84
======================================================================
Play with the D-pad and buttons instead of the stylus (formerly the "D-pad Patch").
Based on the 2014 D-pad patch by griegamaster. Rewritten, relocated and bug-fixed.

Patch target
------------
Legend of Zelda, The - Phantom Hourglass (USA), game code AZEE, rev 0, untrimmed 64 MiB
  SHA-1  4c8f52dd719918bbcd46e73a8bae8628139c1b85
  CRC32  8b431c41
Result:
  SHA-1  8915d99da2dd99bd2c05bc7a79fd2660a5e8ce5e
  CRC32  5b02447a

Two equivalent patch files are included. Both refuse a wrong source ROM.
  zelda_phantom_hourglass_buttons_v2.1.84.xdelta - xdelta3, Delta Patcher, Rom Patcher JS
  zelda_phantom_hourglass_buttons_v2.1.84.bps   - Floating IPS (flips), Rom Patcher JS, beat
Apply to a CLEAN ROM, not to one already patched with an earlier version.
Emulator save states made with an older version carry that version's code: load your
in-game save after updating instead.

Controls
--------
  D-pad              Move (run)
  Y + D-pad          Walk slowly
  A                  Talk / read / pick up / open (searches a few spots in front of Link)
  A (carrying)       Throw, straight in the direction Link faces / you last pressed
                     (also while running). A bomb (from the bag or a bomb flower): running
                     + A throws it, standing A sets it down in front of Link
  A + D-pad          Roll
  A (grabbing block) Push. D-pad toward the block + A = push, D-pad away + A = pull
  A or B             Advance text (taps the text box)
  B                  Wide slash
  B + D-pad          Long slash
  Y + B              Spin attack
  A (hold)           "RUB!" shows (an enemy, green blobs or ice hold Link, or swords locked in a clash): rub
                     free (the stylus rubs back and forth over Link while you hold A)
  A (hold)           Standing on a grappling hook post: jump down the post's own way, with or
                     without the D-pad. Link jumps after about half a second.
  R                  Open/close the item bar
  X                  Take the equipped item out (it is not used, so you can also draw a
                     path with the stylus)
  A (item out)       Use the item (boomerang / bow / hook ...: thrown or shot the way Link
                     faces or you last pressed; shovel: dig; bombs: see A holding). With
                     the bow out, the D-pad only aims; A shoots
  A (hold, hook out) The line sweeps out from Link in a moment. The D-pad turns the aim. Let go of A to throw
  A (hold, bow out)  Aim: the camera moves the way you aim, so you can see targets past the
                     screen edge. The D-pad turns the aim (8 ways). Let go of A to shoot
  X, R or B (item out)  Put the item away (the "Put Away" button)
  B                  A screen with a Back arrow at the bottom left (for example a map on a
                     wall): press the Back arrow
  B                  Bombchu map (the screen that swaps in to draw its path): press the Back arrow
  A / B              Bombchu map, once a path is drawn: A = GO!, B = CANCEL (draw the path with the stylus)
  A / B              Letter at a red mailbox: A = OK, B = Rewrite (write the signature with the stylus)
  D-pad Left/Right   Item bar: highlight the next item or potion (empty places are skipped)
  A                  Item bar: equip the highlighted item (or drink the potion: Yes / No)
  B or R             Item bar: close it without changing the item
  Select             Open the menu bar (Save, Collection, Sea Chart, Map; at sea no Map, but
                     two places for sea items (shown as "?" until you have them) and Fish).
                     Not while an item is out
  D-pad Left/Right   Menu bar: move to the next button (Collection first; it wraps around).
                     The selected button shows pressed (its icon drops a little; Save
                     shows no change)
  A                  Menu bar: open the selected screen
  B or Select        Menu bar: close it
  D-pad              Sea Chart screen (the four seas): highlight a sea you own or an island
                     with a map (its name shows). The D-pad moves to the nearest one that
                     way. The first sea, or the one you opened last, is highlighted when
                     the screen appears
  A                  Sea Chart screen: open the highlighted sea's chart or island's map.
                     B there goes back to the four seas
  B                  Sea Chart screen: close it
  Y                  A map or sea chart: switch between Memo (pen) and Erase (the buttons
                     on the left edge). Drawing still needs the stylus
  Up / Down          A dungeon map with floors: show the floor above / below (the floor
                     list opens at the lower left and scrolls when needed). A tap on the
                     map closes the list
  X                  A note with a "Sea Chart" or "Map" button at the bottom right: press it
                     (B is Back there)
  Left / Right       Statue turn screen (a statue with two yellow arrows): turn the statue
                     one step (Left = clockwise, Right = counterclockwise). Hold to keep
                     turning; a press during a turn is done after it. A does nothing there;
                     B is Back
  L                  Goron Temple: swap between Link and Gongoron (the round button at the
                     top centre of the bottom screen). Final boss fight: the hourglass
                     icon (bottom centre): L opens the drawing frame, L again closes it;
                     draw the figure eight with the stylus. Otherwise not used on the field (the
                     map is in the menu bar; with the map open, A/B/R/X/D-pad close it).
                     L / R turn the collection pages, see below
  B (Gongoron)      Goron Temple, playing Gongoron: hold B to curl up and roll (D-pad = the way); in the rupee minigame A or B
  Start              Pause menu (Save)
  Start, Start       Cutscenes: the first Start shows "Skip >>" (as in the game), a second
                     Start while it shows skips the scene. No other button skips
  B or Select        Collection screen: close it
  R / L              Collection screen: Fishing Journal -> Items -> Treasure -> Ship Parts
                     and back (also with the Items screens switched). D-pad Right/Left
                     does the same, except on the Items page
  D-pad              Collection Items page: highlight an item; its name shows. The D-pad
                     moves to the nearest item that way (empty places are skipped), on the
                     half of the page that is on the bottom screen
  Y                  Collection Items page: switch screens (the bottom-right button)
  D-pad Up/Down/A    Collection Treasure / Ship Parts tabs: highlight an entry (the D-pad
                     then moves the highlight over the filled places), A opens it, B goes
                     back (on the Ship Parts list, A shows the Explanation). Up from the
                     top row lets go; then Left/Right switch tabs
  D-pad Up/Down      Collection Ship Parts list (for example Hull): show the previous / next
                     part you own of that kind
  D-pad Up/Down      Dialogue choices and the save menu: move the highlight (the top option
                     is highlighted when they appear; the highlight wraps around)
  A (option lit)     Pick the highlighted option
  D-pad Up/Down      Game over screen: highlight Continue or Save and Quit (Continue is
                     highlighted when it appears); A picks it, B lets go
  A or Start         Title screen ("Touch the screen!")
  D-pad + A          File select menus: the D-pad highlights an entry, A picks it (A with
                     nothing highlighted picks file 1 / Start / Adventure); B is Back
  D-pad Left/Right   File select Options: change the setting of the current row (Up/Down
                     picks the row; the game shows no highlight, the top row comes first)
  D-pad Up / Down    Sailing: Go / Stop
  A                  Sailing: Jump
  B                  Sailing: fire the cannon straight ahead
  X                  Sailing: open the Routes screen (the Route button)
  D-pad Up / Down    Sailing in the fog with the ship's wheel (Ghost Ship search): Go / Stop
  D-pad Left/Right   Sailing with the ship's wheel: turn (hold; the longer you hold, the
                     harder the turn, full turn after about 2 seconds; let go = straight)
  B                  Routes screen: close it (the back arrow). After you draw a route:
                     CANCEL (erases the route)
  A                  Routes screen, after you draw a route: GO!
  B                  Cyclone Slate: close it (the back arrow). Drawing the symbol still
                     needs the stylus
  X                  Cyclone Slate: open the Sea Chart screen (the four seas)
  A                  Fishing: set the hook when "PULL!" shows (a stylus flick)
  Down (hold)        Fishing: pull up on the rod, to make the gauge rise (stylus slides down).
                     Down + Left / Down + Right slide down-left / down-right
  B (hold)           Fishing, with a fish on the line: reel it in (stylus circles)
  (let go)           Fishing, when "LET GO!" shows: release all buttons (the stylus is off)
  B or Select        Fishing, with no fish on the line: stop fishing (the red arrow)
  D-pad              Shops: move the highlight between the items on the table (the
                     highlighted item is lifted; sold-out places are skipped)
  A                  Shops: open the highlighted item. In the item view, Left/Right
                     picks Buy or Quit, A presses it, Up/Down shows the previous /
                     next item
  B                  Shops: Quit (in the item view: back to the table)
  D-pad Left/Right   Treasure merchant (Mercay, buys treasure and ship parts): switch between
                     the Treasure and Ship Parts tabs. The D-pad then highlights an entry,
                     as on the collection screen
  A                  Treasure merchant: open the highlighted entry (on the Ship Parts tab,
                     its list: Up/Down shows the previous / next part you own, A opens it)
  A                  Treasure merchant, when Sell and Quit show: Sell (Sell is highlighted)
  B                  Treasure merchant: Quit (in a list or the Sell view: go back; on the
                     tabs: leave)
  D-pad              Magic box (stow a treasure or a ship part): as the treasure merchant.
                     Left/Right switch the tabs, the D-pad highlights an entry, A opens it
  A / X              Magic box Ship Parts list: Stow / Explanation; B goes back
  A                  Magic box treasure entry: Stow (Stow is highlighted); B goes back
  B                  Magic box tabs: leave (the red back arrow)
  D-pad Up/Down      Shipyard menu: highlight Customize or View Ship (the button you used
                     last, first Customize, is highlighted when the menu appears)
  A                  Shipyard menu: open the highlighted screen
  B                  Shipyard menu: leave the shipyard (the back arrow)
  D-pad              Shipyard Customize: highlight a part type (the part shows on the ship
                     on the top screen); A opens its list, B goes back to the menu
  D-pad Up/Down      Shipyard parts list: show the previous / next part you own of that kind
  A / X              Shipyard parts list: Attach / Explanation; B goes back
  D-pad Left/Right   Shipyard View Ship: turn the ship
  D-pad Up/Down      Shipyard View Ship: zoom in / out; B goes back to the menu
  D-pad Left/Right   Salvage Arm: move the lever handle (hold; the handle stays where you
                     let go, as with the stylus). The arm swings over to it
  Up / Down (hold)   Salvage Arm: knob up / down, the arm sinks slower / faster
  A                  Salvage Arm: move the handle back to the center
  B or Select        Salvage Arm: stop (the red arrow)
The stylus still works for everything.

English text fixes
------------------
The patch also corrects the English dialogue (since v2.1.55): 7 typos and 26 small
wording mistakes. For example, "the top sceen" in the fishing tutorial is now "the top
screen". These changes affect only the text. Full list of text fixes:
https://github.com/swyfter/phantom-hourglass-button-controls/blob/main/docs/dialogue-typos.md

What changed from v2.1.83 (in v2.1.84)
--------------------------------------
 - Final boss fight, sword clash ("RUB!" while the swords are locked): hold A now wins the
   clash. In v2.1.83 the rub did nothing here, because the game reads the buttons every
   frame in this fight and the rub changed side on every read. Each side of the rub now
   lasts 2 frames, however often the game reads the buttons.

 - Internal cleanup only: the patch code is smaller (about 340 bytes), which leaves room
   for new features. The controls work as in v2.1.31.

What changed from v2.1.30 (in v2.1.31)
--------------------------------------
 - Select opens the menu bar (Save, Collection, Sea Chart, Map). Collection is selected
   first; D-pad Left/Right moves the selection and wraps around from Save to Map; A opens
   that screen; B or Select closes the bar. After a screen from the bar closes, you are
   back on the field with the bar closed. Before, Select opened the collection screen.
 - L no longer opens the map (use Menu bar > Map). L now does nothing.

What changed from v2.1.29 (in v2.1.30)
--------------------------------------
 - B and Y + B no longer swing the sword or spin attack when the stylus could not do it,
   for example while a map on a wall opens and while its text is shown. Before, the first
   B on that text also made Link swing his sword and shout, and Y + B made him spin.
   B still advances the text and presses the Back arrow there, as before.

What changed from v2.1.24 (in v2.1.29; v2.1.25 to v2.1.28 were test builds only)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
 - Bombs: a bomb from a bomb flower now works like a bag bomb. Running + A throws it the
   way Link runs. Standing A sets the bomb down in front of Link, the way he faces (before,
   standing A threw it). Pots, rocks and barrels are still thrown by standing A.
 - B puts away an item that is out (shovel, boomerang, bombs, ...), as the "Put Away"
   button does. Before, no button put the item away. Pressing B quickly again does not take
   the item out again.
 - A with an item out uses it, the same as R. Before, A with the shovel out dug diagonally
   when Link faced left or right. Repeated digs go the same way as the first one.
 - B presses a Back arrow at the bottom left of a screen, for example on a map on a wall.
   Before, B swung the sword behind the map.

What changed from v2.1.23 (in v2.1.24)
--------------------------------------
 - Throwing while running: A throws a pot, rock or barrel while Link runs with the D-pad,
   the way he runs. Before, A did nothing until Link stood still.
 - Bombs: with a bomb out (R), A throws it, running or standing, in all 8 directions.
   Before, a throw to the left or right set the bomb down at Link's feet.

What changed from v2.1.22 (in v2.1.23)
--------------------------------------
 - File select Options: Left / Right change the settings on buttons. Before, a stylus
   press left over from another menu could lock them, so only B worked.

What changed from v2.1.21 (in v2.1.22)
--------------------------------------
 - Collection screen, Ship Parts list (for example the Hull list): D-pad Up / Down shows
   the previous / next part you own of that kind. The list scrolls by itself when the part
   is not on screen. On the first or last part you own, the list stays as it is.

What changed from v2.1.20 (in v2.1.21)
--------------------------------------
 - Collection screen, Treasure and Ship Parts tabs: with an entry highlighted, Left / Right
   at the end of a row now stay on the entry (before, they let go and changed the tab).
   Down on the bottom row stays too. Up on the top row lets go of the entry (also Up from
   the bottom row when the top row is empty); then Left / Right switch tabs as before.

What changed from v2.1.19 (in v2.1.20)
--------------------------------------
 - Collection screen, Items page: Y presses the switch-screens button (bottom right), which
   swaps the top and bottom halves of the page. Y again swaps them back.

What changed from v2.1.18 (in v2.1.19)
--------------------------------------
 - R uses the equipped item: it takes the item out if needed and throws / shoots it the
   way Link faces, or the way you last pressed the D-pad. Bombs: the first R takes one
   out, the next R throws it. The bombchu comes out on R; steer it with the stylus.
 - L opens and closes the map (R did before). Your controller's left trigger is never
   needed.
 - Select opens the collection screen again (as in the original game); B or Select closes
   it. With an entry highlighted on the Treasure / Ship Parts tabs, B lets go of it first.
   The menu bar has no button now (tap it, or use Start for Save).
 - Item bar (X): D-pad Left/Right highlights an item or potion (skipping empty places),
   A equips the item or asks to drink the potion, B or X closes the bar unchanged.

What changed from v2.1.17 (in v2.1.18)
--------------------------------------
 - Collection screen, Treasure and Ship Parts tabs: Up, Down or A highlights an entry
   (the one you looked at last, or the first one). The D-pad moves the highlight and
   skips empty places; A opens the entry, B closes it again (the same entry stays
   highlighted). On the Ship Parts list A shows the Explanation, B goes back.
 - With an entry highlighted, Left / Right past the last filled place in the row lets go
   of it and goes on to the next tab, as before. With nothing highlighted, Left / Right
   switch tabs as in v2.1.17. L or Select still close the collection.

What changed from v2.1.16 (in v2.1.17)

   Right goes Items -> second page -> Ship Parts; Left goes Ship Parts -> Treasure ->
   Items. The second page opens on the tab you used last, as in the original game.

What changed from v2.1.13 (in v2.1.16; v2.1.14 and v2.1.15 were test builds only)
---------------------------------------------------------------------------------
 - Shops: the merchant's window works on buttons. The D-pad moves a highlight between the
   items on the table (the highlighted item is lifted up; sold-out places, also ones you
   just bought, are skipped), A opens it. In the item view Buy is highlighted; Left/Right
   picks Buy or Quit, A presses it (A pressed in the first moment is ignored, so mashing
   A can't buy by accident), Up/Down goes to the previous / next item, B is Quit. On the
   table B is Quit and leaves the shop. After "You don't have enough Rupees!" A or B
   taps Quit.

What changed from v2.1.10 (in v2.1.12; v2.1.11 was a test build only)
---------------------------------------------------------------------
 - Dialogue choices and the save menu (Continue / Save and Quit): the top option is
   highlighted as soon as they appear, so A picks it with no D-pad press. A pressed in
   the first moment (about 0.3 s) is ignored, so mashing A through text can't pick it.
 - Up/Down move the highlight about twice as fast, and wrap around (Down on the last
   option goes to the first, Up on the first goes to the last). Presses made while the
   highlight is still moving are no longer lost.
 - Save menu: B (Continue) no longer makes Link talk to or touch whatever is behind the
   menu after it closes.

What changed from v2.1.7 (in v2.1.9; v2.1.8 was a test build only)
------------------------------------------------------------------
 - Title screen: A or Start goes on to file select.
 - File select: Up/Down picks a file (or Nintendo WFC Setup), Left/Right picks Copy /

   now arrives on the next frame, as in the original game.

What changed from v2.1.2 (in v2.1.3)
------------------------------------
 - Buttons are ignored again whenever the game itself ignores them (cutscenes and similar
   scenes), so they can't move Link or tap things there. A and B still advance text.
 - Throwing after lifting something from the side goes straight, the way Link picked it
   up (before, without pressing the D-pad first, the throw could go diagonally).

What changed from v2.1.1 (in v2.1.2)
------------------------------------
 - Push blocks: while Link holds a block, A taps the push or pull arrow. D-pad toward
   the block + A pushes, D-pad away + A pulls, A alone pushes. Before, only pull worked,
   and pressing the D-pad made Link let go of the block.
 - After Link lets go of a block, A aims at it again (until you use the D-pad), so it
   grabs the block instead of swinging at the air.
 - A + D-pad doesn't roll while holding a block.

What changed from v2.1 (in v2.1.1)
----------------------------------
 - L now closes the items screen as well as opening it. In v2.1 you had to press Select
   to close it.

What changed from v2 (in v2.1)
------------------------------
 - A aims where you last pressed the D-pad (for 1.5 s), not just Link's body heading,
   so it works even before Link has finished turning.
 - A now tries up to 5 spots in front of Link (7 when facing up), one after another,
   and stops as soon as the game reacts. Talking from a distance, lifting rocks and
   barrels, and reading signs Ciela points at now work from below as well as from the
   sides and above.
 - Throwing: A while carrying something sends one straight tap, so throws go where
   Link faces (before, extra taps could bend a sideways throw diagonally).
 - L and Select swapped: Select = menu bar, L = items screen.

Known limitations
-----------------
 - Lifting from directly below works about 19 times in 20; if Link only steps forward,
   press A again.
 - A aims items straight ahead only: a boomerang path, the bombchu path and aiming at a
   point still need the stylus. Hammer is not tested yet.
 - Sailing: drawing a route, aiming the cannon at a point and turning the camera still
   need the stylus. B pressed while the Routes screen is still opening does nothing.
 - Fishing: the rod is pulled down the screen only (straight or diagonal). The "?" places on the sea
   menu bar are stops where A does nothing until you get those items.
 - Treasure merchant: for about 1.5 seconds after the screen opens, or after a list closes
   while he talks, the game ignores the buttons. Press again.
 - Microphone spots ("talk into the mic"): the patch does not change them. In RetroArch with
   the melonDS DS core, set Microphone Input Mode to Blow (Core Options > Audio) and hold
   the Microphone button (L3 by default) at the prompt.
